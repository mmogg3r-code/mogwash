Our Services

Paint Protection Film

  • Rock chip protection
  • Self-healing glossy finish
  • UV and stain defense

Paint protection film adds an invisible layer over your vehicle’s paint to defend it from rock chips, scratches, bird droppings, and UV fading while keeping a deep, glossy shine and helping maintain resale value.

Ceramic Coating

  • Lasting gloss
  • Water repellent
  • UV protection

Ceramic coating bonds permanently to your vehicle’s paint, creating a hard, hydrophobic shield that repels water, dirt, and UV rays — keeping your car looking showroom-fresh for years with minimal upkeep.

Window Tint

  • Heat rejection
  • UV blockage
  • Enhanced privacy

Window tinting blocks heat, glare, and harmful UV rays while giving your vehicle a sleek, private look — keeping your interior cooler and protected from sun damage year-round.

Scratch Removal

  • Paint restored
  • Swirl mark removal
  • Showroom finish

Our scratch removal service eliminates surface scratches, swirl marks, and paint scuffs — restoring your vehicle’s finish to a smooth, flawless look without the cost of a full repaint.

Car Detailing

  • Deep interior clean
  • Exterior polish
  • Odour elimination

Our full car detailing service restores your vehicle inside and out, from deep interior cleaning and odour removal to exterior polish and paint decontamination, leaving your car looking and feeling brand new.
